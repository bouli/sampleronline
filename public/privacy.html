privacy.

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Your audio is processed on your device. The sampler and synthesizer do not upload your sounds, patterns, or saved synth patches to our servers.

Sounds and sessions

Files you choose or drop into the sampler are read in your browser. Trimming, envelopes, sequencing, synthesis, and effects also run there. We do not have an account system or a server-side library of your sounds.

Storage in your browser

The app uses IndexedDB, a browser database, to store saved session data, including audio, sample and session names, patterns, settings, and your My sounds synth library. It also uses this storage to move a synthesized sample between pages.

This data is specific to the browser and site address you use. It is not automatically synchronized with another browser or device. Browser settings, private browsing, storage limits, or clearing site data can remove it.

To delete the app’s locally stored data, use your browser’s settings to clear site data for sampleronline.com. Export anything you want to keep first. Clearing site data does not delete files you have already downloaded.

Exports and backups

Export Session creates a ZIP on your device containing your samples, patterns, settings, names, and saved synth sounds. Import Session reads a ZIP locally and asks before replacing your current session and synth library. Save WAV also creates a local download.

We do not keep a server backup or offer recovery of lost browser data. Keep exported session ZIPs somewhere safe and use Import Session to restore them later. Anyone you share a session ZIP with can access its contents.

Cookies and analytics

The application does not set cookies or include analytics, advertising trackers, or behavioral profiling code. Browser storage is used for the music-making features described above.

Page requests and hosting

Loading the site sends normal web requests to its hosting infrastructure. Those requests include information such as your IP address, requested URL, and browser information. Hosting providers may process technical access or security logs; their logging and retention depend on the hosting configuration. These page requests do not contain your local samples or session contents.

Google Fonts

The site loads DM Sans and Space Grotesk from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore makes requests to Google’s font services, which receive information such as your IP address and browser details. Your audio and session contents are not sent with those requests. See Google Fonts’ privacy information.

External links and support

The developer link opens GitHub, and “buy me a coffee” opens Ko-fi. Following those links takes you to services with their own privacy policies. Any support payment is handled by Ko-fi and its payment providers; this app does not collect your payment details.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, contact the developer through GitHub. Please do not post private audio or session files publicly. If the app’s data practices change, this page and its update date should be revised.
